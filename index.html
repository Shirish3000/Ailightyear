Exposure

How much of your work can AI do?

Pick the kinds of work that fill a normal week, or add your own. Give each one rough hours, then pick how much of it AI could do well enough that you’d accept the result. Be honest; nobody’s checking your timesheet.

Representation

How well does AI know your world?

Think about the AI tools you’ve used, or could use, for your own work.

Something about your world we haven’t asked? Add it and answer it the same way.

Not sure? Ask an AI tool two or three real questions from your work, in the language you actually use, and see how it does. Think of it as a mirror test.

If AI changed your work tomorrow, what would you have to fall back on?

Something else you could fall back on, or would lack? Add it and answer it the same way.

What else shapes your distance

Optional, and it won’t change your scores.

Your dials show where you stand today. These questions look at the currents around you that can move them. Each answer adds a short note to your reading. Answer any that apply.

Something niche shaping your distance that we haven’t asked about? Add it and say which way it pushes.

There are no questions about your age, gender or education. Studies do find group differences in AI use, but averages describe groups, not you. The notes draw on Bick et al. (2026), NBER Working Paper 34995, and the BCG Henderson Institute (2026).

Your reading

Your personal AI year

A light-year is the distance light travels in a year. Your AI year is the distance AI travels toward you in a year. Save this reading, come back in about six months, and this space will show how much closer AI came, and whether your footing kept pace.

Only your scores and the date are saved, in this browser. Your tasks and answers are never stored.

Distance and footing
ReadingBuilt fromWhat it means
Distance10 minus the average of Exposure and RepresentationHow far AI is from you. 10 is far away; 0 is right next to you.
FootingThe average of Access and PurposeHow ready you are. 10 is solid ground; 0 is very little.
Personal AI yearChange in distance between two saved readings, scaled to a yearHow far AI travelled toward you in a year, shown next to how much your footing changed.

Scores of 0–3 count as low and 7–10 as high, with 4–6 in the middle. These are rough guides rather than fixed thresholds.

Something as rudimentary and simple as these four dials is just one take on how such a measure might begin. It isn’t a finished or validated method, and it’s meant to be questioned, tested and improved.
